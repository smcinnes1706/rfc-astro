On Sunday 20th July, Rotherfield FC host 'Get Ready' Day.

Jobs to be done

- Stain shed with green fence paint
- Rub down, treat rust & repaint with Hammerite on the mini soccer goals
- Hammerite the aluminium 11 a side & 9v9 goals
- Tidy shed
- Clean down external club house walls & touch up masonry paint

Paint provided as much as possible but bring your own brushes

If you can spare a couple of hours to get ready for the 2025/26 season then please come along.
