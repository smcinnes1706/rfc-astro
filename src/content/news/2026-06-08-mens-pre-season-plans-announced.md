Preparations for the new season at Rotherfield Football Club are edging ever closer with the club’s full senior pre-season programme being announced. Sessions start up once again on Tuesday the 30th of June at the Rotherfield Recreation Ground from 18:00-20:00 with sessions running twice a week, every Tuesday and Thursday, until the season starts.

The first week of sessions, Tuesday the 30th of June and Thursday 2nd July, will see the club hold two open training sessions where all prospective new players are invited to attend alongside the club’s existing players. This will afford prospective new players the opportunity to sample the club and impress the side’s management. All prospective new players wishing to join the first week’s open training sessions are advised to contact First Team Manager, Wayne Clarke, on 07540 765685.

All pre-season sessions will be led by the First Team Management Team, Wayne Clarke and Callum Ridley, with other qualified club coaches supporting the sessions too. Training will be supported by the First Team’s announced pre-season friendly programme that sees six fixtures taking place before the season starts proper. The club’s Reserve Team pre-season fixtures, now being led by Richard Holman, are in the final stages of being agreed and will be announced shortly.

The 2026/27 campaign looks set to be a strong one for all Rotherfield FC members with all three senior sides, Firsts, Reserves and Veterans, buoyed by excellent finishes to the previous campaign.

We look forward to seeing as many of you as possible from Tuesday the 30th of June as possible!
