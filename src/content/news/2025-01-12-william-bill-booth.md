Rotherfield Football Club are saddened to hear of the passing of former player and Sussex Referee William 'Bill' Booth on Saturday 11th January.

Bill was a long time supporter of the club as well as playing for the Greens in the 1950's making 106 appearances and scoring 18 goals between 1954 to 1959.

Rotherfield FC send Bill's family their condolences at this time.
