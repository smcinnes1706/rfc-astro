Wayne Clarke, Alex Watts and Callum Ridley have confirmed as First Team management team for 2025/26 season.

The Club are pleased to confirm the First Team management team for 2025/26 season.

> Good progress was made last season & now we want to build on that to achieve greater consistency

Chairman Pete Ford provided rfc1893.com  the following statement; “It is important to have continuity in management & so the club is delighted that Wayne Clarke, Alex Watts & Calum Ridley will be the team to lead the Firsts next season."

"Good progress was made last season & now we want to build on that to achieve greater consistency. I know these guys will work very hard to provide a special football experience for players & I already know they are working on focused recruitment. By resolving managerial appointments before last season is dry provides them the greatest chance of achieving our ambitious objectives.”
