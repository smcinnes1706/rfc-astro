This Saturday, Rotherfield face Hollington United at the Recreation Ground.

Last Time Out

Last week, Hollington were victorious in a 2 - 1 win over Holland Sports at The Rise Electrical Gibbons Memorial Ground. Toby Payne and Harrison Smith netted for the Lions.

Previous Meeting

Hollington United have been side that has been in and out of the Mid-Sussex League over the last few seasons. Last season Hollington won the East Sussex Football League and now play National Feeder League Football in the MSFL Premier Division.

Our last meeting was back on 19th April 2023 at Rotherfield where the visitors won

1 -5 in an evening fixture.  Hollington went on to win the MSFL Premier Division that season. Zach McEniry is the only player in the current Lions squad that featured in our last meeting with Hollington, who provided our only goal.

Who To Watch

Both Toby Payne and Harrison Smith are on 2 goals each so they maybe a threat to watch out for. Last season's leading goal scorer Archie Baker has yet to find the net this season.

Match Details

Venue: Rotherfield Recreation Ground, North Street, TN6 3LX

KO: 2pm

Facilities: Clubhouse open for refreshments.

Match Referee: Mark Parsons
