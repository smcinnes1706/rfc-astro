Rotherfield Junior FC Under 7s member Henry is taking part in Great Ormond Street Hospital Charity’s family festival and fun run, RBC Race for the Kids, to raise money to help build the new Children’s Cancer Centre at Great Ormond Street Hospital (GOSH).

If you would like to donate to support our Henry in the this great cause, then follow the link below.

https://race.gosh.org/fundraisers/antoniaadair/rbc-race-for-the-kids

> "I want to race to help other children who are sick. I hope I can run fast and raise as much money as I can!" - Henry Under 7s
