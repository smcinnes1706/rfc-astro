We will have a morning working party on Sunday 28 July to get things ready at the club for the rapidly approaching new season.

From 10am & bring with you paint brushes, spades, ladders, wheelbarrows etc

Plan is to brush off shed & paint; sand goal posts & paint (who loves keepers bashing their studs against the posts!!), tidy up around club, clean player shelters & paint benches, clean Football Foundation sign on entrance to the ground plus anything else you want to see done.

Many hands will make light work so please do help with getting your club ready!

No lightweights or excuses!
