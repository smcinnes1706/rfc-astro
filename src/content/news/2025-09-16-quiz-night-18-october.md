Rotherfield Junior FC will be holding a Quiz Night to raise funds for the club's winter training facilities.

This is a chance to have a laugh and enjoy a few drinks and maybe get a few questions right too!

There will be only one round of football questions and the rest is general knowledge. The winning team will get a £100 cash prize, Runner-up team will get a £50 cash prize and third place team will be able to get a free drink for each player on that team.
