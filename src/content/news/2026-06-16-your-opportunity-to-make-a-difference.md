Rotherfield Football club has been at the heart of the village community for 133 years. Whilst the teams & playing football successfully is the primary focus, the club is the engine that powers this journey and we want your help.

Do you have enthusiasm for football, a determination to power the club forward & do you have ideas to innovate the face of football for the better?

If you do then join our leadership team to deliver significant change?

No football skills or previous experience required & the details of your role can be finalised once you & more of the right people are involved! This is an open invitation to everyone, with a desire to help a thriving community club do even more great work as we get fit for the future.

Contact Pete Ford, Chair person to discuss this opportunity & what could work for you  (petegford@outlook.com or 07714837297)
