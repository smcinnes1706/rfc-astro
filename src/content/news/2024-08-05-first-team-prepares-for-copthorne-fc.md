Due to our original opponents now not being able to raise a side, this Saturday our First Team now welcomes new fellow Premier Division team Tunbridge Wells U23s to the Recreation Ground as the Greens continue their pre-season preparations.

This is the first time the Greens will play at home this season and the ground looks fantastic thanks to the tireless efforts of our grounds team.

Pre-season has gone well with new head coach Wayne Clarke and assistant Alex Watts overseeing two fixtures and four weeks of training where they've had the opportunity to see both current and new players.

So far everyone has worked hard and impressed overall ready for a tougher test this weekend.

Come and join us on Saturday afternoon at Rotherfield Recreation Ground for a 2pm KO. Bar open after the game.
