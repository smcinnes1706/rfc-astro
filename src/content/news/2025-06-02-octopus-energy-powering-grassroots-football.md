Grassroot Media and Octopus Energy have teamed up to support Grassroots Football.

This is part of an ongoing campaign to help clubs earn money through the Octopus Energy's referral scheme.

The referral scheme itself:

- Our club earns £50 for every person who switches to Octopus Energy using your unique referral link https://octopus.energy/peoplepower/octokit-teams/
- and search for Rotherfield Sports Club
- You get £50 off their energy bill
- There’s no cost, no obligation and no minimum target – just sign up
