Crowborough & District Junior Football League held their presentation evening at the Uckfield Civic Centre on Friday 14 June.

A number of teams from Rotherfield Junior FC received awards which the club are immensely proud of.

Our Under 11 Rovers won the Fair Play Award for Division F, our Under 12 Raptors received the League Shield Tournament, the Under 12 Rangers also received the Fair Play award for Division D and our Under 15s picked up their League Winners trophy.
