This Saturday our firsts travel to Mill Lane Recreation Ground, home of Holland Sports.

Last Time Out

The Sports were in action last week to Lindfield but were on the receiving end of a defeat.

Previous Meeting

Our last meeting was also at Mill Lane where the Rother lost 2-0, the highlight of the afternoon was a good first team debut for the then 16 year old Jacob Prince. Previous to that, Rotherfield beat Holland Sports at home where Jack Spampanato scored a hat-trick.

Who To Watch

Sam Cockerill, Todlucas Scott and John Hill are certainly ones to keep an eye on tomorrow. Last season, these three players had a combined goal tally of 43 goals which is 81% of their total goal tally.

Match Details

MILL LANE RECREATION GROUND, Mill Lane, Hurst Green, OXTED, RH8 9DG

Kick Off: 2pm

Admission: Free

Facilities and Refreshments: Bar and clubhouse available.

Referee: Mark Gidman
