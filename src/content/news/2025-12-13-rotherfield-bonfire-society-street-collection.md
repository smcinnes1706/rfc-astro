RJFC u8 players Archie & Arnie being presented with their certificates of achievement from RFC Chair Pete Ford.

Both lads walked the entire procession with collection buckets to raise money.

RJFC were one of the recipients from the street collection & received a fantastic £500!

Thanks Archie & Arnie (& everyone else who walked) for their efforts! Stars!

The money will be used towards new portable goals for the juniors to facilitate the new FA 3v3 format.
