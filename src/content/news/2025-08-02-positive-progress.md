The first team drew 2-2 with Roffey Reserves on Friday evening. A really promising performance with some excellent debuts from the new guys.

On Saturday the Reserves stormed into a 5-0 lead at half time before eventually winning 6-3 away at Five Oak Green. A Sean Bristow hat trick was one of many highlights.

On Tuesday the first team have a second friendly away v AFC Uckfield 7.30pm KO, & on Thursday the Reserves also play their second friendly away at Wadhurst 6.30pm KO.

Then on Saturday 9th August the firsts are away against Ide Hill.
