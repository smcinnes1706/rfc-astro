Last Sunday, club stalwart Craig King made his 400th appearance for Rotherfield FC, playing for the Vets.

Craig or Kinger, as he is also known as, joined the club at the age of 16 alongside his father Terry and uncle Gary having been a youth at Broad Oak and Horam FC.

Craig's highlights for the club was debuting for the First Team at the age of 16 and being part of the Montgomery Cup Final squad that lost to Peacehaven United on penalties on 10th May 2014.

Kinger not only played for both Reserves and First teams, he has also coached various age groups in our Junior Club.

Rotherfield FC congratulates Craig for his service to the club and we hope you can get to 500!
