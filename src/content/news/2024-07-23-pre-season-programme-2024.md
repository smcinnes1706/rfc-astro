Rotherfield FC have been back at pre-season training for nearly four weeks and preparations take a step up as the teams prepare for their programme of friendlies and interclub matches with the first coming on Saturday 27th July.

FIRST TEAM

Saturday 27 July First Team v Five Oak Green; at Five Oak Green Recreation Ground. KO 2pm

Saturday 3 August First Team v Reserves at Goldsmiths Leisure Centre Crowborough. 1pm meet

Saturday 10 August First Team v Copthorne FC at Rotherfield Recreation Ground, KO 2pm

Saturday 17 August First Team v Hawkenbury FC at Hawkenbury Recreation Ground, KO 2pm

RESERVE TEAM

Tuesday 13 August Reserves v Nutley at Fords Green Recreation Ground, KO 6.30pm

Tuesday 20 August Reserves v Wadhurst United at Washwell Lane KO 6.30pm .

Sunday 1 September Reserves v RJFC U18s at Rotherfield Recreation Ground  KO 10.30am

The First Team start their league programme on 24 August with a home game, 2pm KO (opposition tbc).
