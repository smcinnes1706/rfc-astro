Rotherfield continue their spell of away fixtures with their longest away trip on Saturday to Reigate Priory Reserves.

Reigate currently sit in 8th place, two spots above Rotherfield, winning two and losing three of their five league games.

Last Time Out:

Reigate entertained Crowhurst last week and were on the end of a 0-2 defeat.

Previous Meeting:

Rotherfield have never played Reigate Priory Reserves but have played their first team back in February 2024 when the Greens lost 4-0.

One To Watch

Reigate's Noah Chant is their current leading scorer with 3 goals in 4 matches and netting 16 times in 12 appearances last season.
