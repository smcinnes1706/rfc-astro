Tomorrow, Rotherfield are once again playing hosts, when Hastings based Westfield come to the Recreation Ground.

LAST TIME OUT

Westfield were in midweek action in the Hastings FA Intermediate Cup where they beat Sidley United 8-3, . Previous to that, the Westies beat Shoreham 3-1 last Saturday.

PREVIOUS MEETING

Westfield were our hosts on the opening game of the season where they beat the Greens 1-0 with an early goal from Corey Wheeler.

Rotherfield vs Westfield

Venue: Rotherfield Recreation Ground, North Street, TN6 3LX

Kick Off: 2pm

Referee: Stephen Ellis

Refreshments: Half time tea bar, Bar open after full time

PLEASE NOTE - DOGS ARE NOT ALLOWED ON THE RECREATION GROUND
