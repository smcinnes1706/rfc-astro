Rotherfield FC have now completed their fourth week of pre-season training at Rotherfield Recreation Ground. Despite the recent arid weather leaving the grass areas extremely hard and much of the grass having died off, the squad have continued to put in the hard work, making use of every available area to ensure each session has gone ahead.

The commitment from the players has been outstanding, with more than 30 players regularly attending training each week. The excellent turnout has created a competitive environment, with players pushing each other as preparations continue for the new campaign.

The squad have also played three pre-season friendlies, giving everyone the opportunity to build match fitness, gain valuable minutes on the pitch and impress the management team ahead of the season.

With pre-season progressing well, the focus now turns to being ready for our first league fixture of the season on Saturday 22nd August.

The hard work continues, and we look forward to seeing everyone back at Rotherfield Recreation Ground as the countdown to the new season goes on.
