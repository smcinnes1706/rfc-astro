This Saturday, Rotherfield start their 2025/26 campaign at the Knight & Davey Community Stadium as they take on promotion hopefuls Westfield FC.

ABOUT WESTFIELD

LAST SEASON

Westfield finished 5th last season in a fairly close top five. They won 14 of their 24 league games finishing on 46 points.

VENUE

Westfield entertain their opponents at the recently developed Knight & Davey Community Stadium. A lovely little ground which is just across the road from their previous venue.

Venue Address:

The Knight & Davey Community Stadium, The Tawnies, Main Road, Westfield TN35 4SL

Admission:

Adults: £3, U18s and concessions £1, U16s FREE. Gates open at 12.30pm

Refreshments:

Food van and bar

Kick Off:

2pm

Referee:

Mark Edwards
