Rotherfield Junior FC teams were involved with the annual Jarvis Brook FC football fiesta on the weekend of 6 & 7 July.

Our 13s won their age group (see above) having seen off their fellow RJFC side in the semi-final!

Our U7s and U8s had an enjoyable time playing lots of football and receiving their medals.

For our U10s, it was their first ever tournament and reached the final through an exciting penalty shoot out! Unfortunately, the side lost in the final but did a fantastic job in getting that far and made every one at the club proud.

Our U16s got to the semi-final of their group losing to the tournament hosts. The squad also presented coach Alan with a framed shirt as this engagement marked his official retirement from coaching.

Below are a few photo's and videos kindly taken and provided by coaches and parents of players of the club.

Finally we'd like to extend our thanks to Jarvis Brook for another great weekend.
