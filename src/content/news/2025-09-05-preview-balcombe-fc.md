This Saturday our firsts entertain Balcombe FC at the Recreation Ground.

Last Time Out

Despite having a good start to the league, Balcombe last time out were on the end of a 2-1 defeat to Hollington United.

Previous Meeting

Balcombe did the double over us last season but the last time we faced them was at Downlands College back in January where Alex Pollard netted twice in a 2-0 win.

Who To Watch

Alex Pollard was a prolific goal scorer last season, finding the net on 30 occasions in 31 appearances. He has continued his good form so far scoring 4 goals in three games alongside Tom Hawkins who equals Pollard's tally.

Match Details

Rotherfield Recreation Ground, North Street, TN6 3LX

Kick Off: 2pm

Admission: Free

Facilities and Refreshments: Half-time tea bar in clubhouse available, full bar opens after full-time.

Referee: Stephen Ellis
