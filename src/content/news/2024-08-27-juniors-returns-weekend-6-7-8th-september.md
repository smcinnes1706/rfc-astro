September is around the corner which means that the football season is almost upon us! Ok, well for some its started and some of our age groups have been doing some pre-season sessions and friendlies already.

However Saturday 7th September is when football sessions for our Foundation Phase age groups (primary school years) starts at Rotherfield Recreation Ground. The club is excited to welcome both current and new players to the club and we hope you enjoy the coming season.

Below are the Saturday morning session times for the season:

U6s 9.30 - 10.15am

U7s 9.00 - 10.00am

U8s 9.00 - 10.00am

U9s 10.30 - 11.30am

U10s 10.30 - 11.30am

Don't forget to pop into our clubhouse and visit 4Ds Snack Bar where you can get a cuppa and a snack while enjoying your young ones play.

But that's not all, 7/8th September is also the inaugural weekend of the C&DJFL 2024/25 season with our U11 Red Star traveling to Uckfield Grasshoppers Raiders on the Saturday.

On the Sunday, our U12 Rebels are also away to Uckfield Grasshoppers Rhinos, while the U12 Rovers welcome Tonbridge Invicta Spitfires. The U13 Raptors entertain Uckfield Grasshoppers Hornets. Our 16s have an exciting trip to the home of Tonbridge Angels, THE HALCYON WEALTH LONGMEAD STADIUM, to face Tonbridge Junior Town.  Lastly, our U17s are also in Tonbridge and face Tonbridge Junior Thistle.

However the most exciting news is to come. This season we were delighted to have our first ever all girls team which will compete in the U12 Girls division.  They have their first game on Saturday morning against East Grinstead Meads Girls Real.

Also Friday 6th September sees the return of our Wildcats group at Rotherfield Recreation Ground starting at 5.30pm.

To follow our Junior teams progress in full visit FA Full Time Crowborough & Junior District League and select the division you want to view.
