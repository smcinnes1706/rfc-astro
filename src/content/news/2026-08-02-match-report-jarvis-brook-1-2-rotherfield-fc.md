Pre-Season Friendly

Rotherfield FC claimed an excellent 2-1 victory in their latest pre-season fixture, overcoming local neighbours Jarvis Brook in an entertaining and competitive encounter. Making the short trip to face higher-level opposition just one week before the hosts' season opener, the Greens knew they faced another stern examination of their preparations.

With a number of players unavailable through injury and availability, manager Wayne Clarke took the opportunity to assess several new faces while also looking at players in unfamiliar positions.

The opening exchanges saw Jarvis Brook enjoy slightly more of the possession, although Rotherfield produced some encouraging spells on the ball. Neat interplay between the fluid front three of Finlay MacLeod, Dan Gray and Elliot Cole showed flashes of promise, but the visitors were unable to seriously test the home goalkeeper.

At the other end, James Gurney was the busier of the two keepers. Within the opening 10 minutes he produced an outstanding save with his leg to deny what appeared to be a certain goal. As the half progressed, Jarvis Brook continued to threaten, particularly down Rotherfield's right-hand side, but Gurney demonstrated his reflexes and acrobatic shot-stopping with a series of impressive saves.

The deadlock was eventually broken from a set piece. A free-kick delivered from the right-hand side sailed over the Rotherfield defence and was headed home at the back post to give the hosts a deserved lead.

The goal unsettled the Greens, who struggled to retain possession during the period that followed. Callum Ridley and Alex Kerslake were outstanding at the heart of the defence, ensuring the deficit remained at just one goal.

Midway through the half, Clarke made a couple of tactical changes, introducing Tommy Overton to provide a different focal point in attack and Bailey Mintrim into the left side of the defence. The adjustments helped Rotherfield regain their composure, and despite trailing 1-0 at the break, there were plenty of positives to build on.

The management team called for calm during the interval, believing greater composure in possession would allow the visitors to make their attacking quality count.

That proved to be the case almost immediately after the restart. Excellent vision from Ridley released Finlay MacLeod with a perfectly weighted through ball. Showing real composure, the forward took his time before calmly slotting beyond the advancing goalkeeper to bring Rotherfield level.

The equaliser lifted the visitors, with the midfield led superbly by captain Sam Saunders beginning to take control. Dan Gray, who had looked lively throughout, was then fouled on the edge of the penalty area, giving Rotherfield a dangerous free-kick.

In a moment mirroring Jarvis Brook's opener, Alex Kerslake delivered an inviting cross into the box and Tommy Overton rose highest to power a header beyond the goalkeeper and complete the turnaround.

Jarvis Brook responded strongly, probing forward with increasing urgency and creating several dangerous opportunities. Gurney continued his outstanding afternoon with a string of important saves, while young full-backs Archie Russell and Bailey Mintrim coped admirably with sustained pressure in the wide areas.

The hosts also forced a succession of corners, but despite missing several of their taller players, Rotherfield defended resolutely. The Greens even had opportunities to extend their advantage, with Overton going close to his second goal after striking a powerful effort that flashed narrowly wide.

As the match entered its closing stages, it developed into something more akin to a competitive league derby than a pre-season friendly, with both sides fully committed. Clarke freshened things up with further substitutions, and a determined defensive display over the closing stages ensured Rotherfield held firm to secure an impressive 2-1 victory.

Attention now turns to Tuesday 11 August, when the Greens welcome a youthful Crowborough Athletic side to Rotherfield Recreation Ground for their next pre-season fixture as preparations continue ahead of the 2026/27 season.

Line-up: Gurney; Sellings, Ridley, Kerslake, Russell; Saunders, Handford, Dunn; MacLeod, Gray, Cole.

Substitutes (all used): Overton, Mintrim, Chittenden, Howard.
