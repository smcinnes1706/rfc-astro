Mike Peevor on the ball. Image credit Will Hugall

Rotherfield will enjoy the pleasures of another home game this time versus Cuckfield Rangers. After lots of work on the pitch during the week the surface should be excellent (weather permitting) & the players should be confident after a very good performance last week albeit in a narrow defeat. Room for improvement!

The side is virtually unchanged & the management team are looking forward to further progression from the likes of Cameron Routledge, Archie Russel, Ethan Laughton-Zimmerman & especially George Fyffe following their levels of last week.

Training this week had a real buzz of quality with infectious displays from Peevor & Teague from the more experienced players & equally influential performances from Jacob & Jasper at the other end of the age range!

Fixture: Rotherfield vs Cuckfield Rangers

Venue: Rotherfield Recreation Ground, North Street, TN6 3LX

Kick Off: 2pm

Referee: Nick Greenfield

Half-Time refreshments available, bar open after Full Time.

PLEASE NOTE: NO DOGS ALLOWED ON THE RECREATION GROUND
