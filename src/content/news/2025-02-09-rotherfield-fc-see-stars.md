Rotherfield Football Club have renewed their England Football Accreditation. The great news is that we have increased our star rating to 2 stars!

WHAT IS ENGLAND FOOTBALL ACCREDITED?

England Football Accredited is a framework for Club Accreditation by the England & Wales Football Association that is a mark of excellence in everything that a football club endeavours to achieve.

It is the way The FA recognises those Clubs that raise the bar and set the tempo for football to thrive in England, creating maximum opportunities for everyone who enjoys the game to participate.

WHAT IS THE SIGNIFICANCE OF TWO STARS?

It reflects the fact that we offer two of the three pathways for football participation as defined by The FA: male & female.

This is a fantastic achievement and everyone should celebrate the renewal of this mark of excellence & the continued progression of our club.
