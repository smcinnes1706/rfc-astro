Friday 8th May, Rotherfield Football Club hosted the annual Awards Night.

Dan Taylor was BBQ chef for the evening and Stuart McInnes and Andy Garrett set up the 4 -a-side footgolf tournament., with the winning side featuring Jack Turner, Nathan Milroy, Sam Saunders and Richard Clibbens (below)

Club Chairman Pete Ford opened the formal proceedings thanking everyone who had made this season possible.

Young Player Of The Year Archie Russell receiving the Josh Alexander #21 Award being presented by Pete Ford for his consistent performances in the First Team.

The 'Significant Goal' Award in memory of Aaron King being presented to Jack Birdsall. This was presented in recognition of his first senior goal at the club against Southwick which then cemented his place in the Reserve team.

Veterans Joint Players' Player of the Season Richard Clibbens, being presented his award by Veterans Chairman Stuart 'Jocky' McInnes

Reserve Team Manager's Player Of The Year was awarded to James Bowen.

Reserve Team Players' Player of the Year went to Seb Chick.

First Team Manager's Player of the Year and Players' Player of the Year went to John Sinclair.

Clubperson of the Year was awarded to Dilan Nlendi for his commitment to playing and training.
