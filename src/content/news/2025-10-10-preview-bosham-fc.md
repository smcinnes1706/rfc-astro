This week our First Team welcome Bosham FC in the Sussex County FC Intermediate Challenge Cup 1st round.

Bosham are based west of Chichester so we hope they have a safe journey up to the Recreation Ground. The Robins compete in Division 2 of the Southern Combination League.

Last Time Out

Bosham played away to Southwater last week in a difficult contest for them. Bosham lost 0-8.

Previous Meeting

Rotherfield played Bosham in the same competition back in February 2023. The Greens had a scratch side and were made to bow out of the competition losing 4-1. John Penny netting for the Greens as stand in striker. Despite the defeat, Rotherfield received a very warm welcome and were looked after very well.

Fixture Details

Rotherfield vs Bosham FC

Venue: Rotherfield Recreation Ground, TN6 3LX

Kick off: 2pm

Referee: John Elms

Halftime refreshments available. Bar open after match.
